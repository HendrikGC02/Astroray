2026-08-06 · restructure run · commits 78b451b, 50b1d93 on main · generated by run-report

The measurement phase found the real bottlenecks were not where expected: local builds were fully serial and couldn't even target your GPU natively, and 56% of all CI compute was spent on docs-only commits. Both are now fixed and verified: cold builds dropped 320.7 s → 61.1 s (5.2×), docs pushes will skip the CI compile matrix entirely, and the opencode delegation layer is live with three verified worker tiers behind an evidence contract.
