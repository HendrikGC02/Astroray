GPU Spectral-Caustic Quality Round

Fixing the two hands-on caustic observations: frozen noise, and rainbow-instead-of-emission-lines.

2026-08-26 · architect-led overnight run · generated by run-report

Three packages shipped and merged, closing both caustic problems the owner flagged from live Blender inspection. pkg220 makes the GPU photon caustic actually average down — it was rebuilding a byte-identical photon map every progressive iteration, so its grain was frozen forever. pkg221 importance-samples each photon's wavelength from the emitting light's spectral power distribution, so a sodium lamp now casts an amber emission-line caustic instead of a physically-impossible continuous rainbow. pkg222 then corrects the stored mercury-lamp spectrum (it was magenta; real mercury is greenish-white) so those emission-line caustics render the right colour. All three are HW-verified on the RTX 5070 Ti and re-verified together on a fresh main build. Nothing failed; pkg223 (Normal Map) is parked as the next pickup — the cheap-model tier filed its research note but stalled on the register-hostile implementation.

2Visual evidence

pkg221 — emission-line dispersion

Sodium lamp → amber. Photons cluster at the 589 nm D-line; chroma (0.824, 0.176, 0.000). This was a full-band rainbow before pkg221.
Broadband white lamp → neutral (0.337, 0.337, 0.325). Confirms the change is SPD-driven, not a global tint; white scenes are unchanged.

pkg222 — mercury lamp colour

Before: magenta — stored SPD (0.354, 0.300, 0.346), blue>green, yellow doublet missing. Wrong for a mercury lamp.
After: greenish-white (0.360, 0.389, 0.251), green-dominant — matches a real clear high-pressure mercury lamp.
