Colour, light and volumes: eight PRs, one observer bug

Astroray lead session — 2026-09-19 23:00 → 2026-09-20 midday AEST (Claude Opus 5, five lanes, two usage-limit kills)

2026-09-20 · lead session · generated by run-report

Eight PRs landed and the RTX sweep closed green at 899 passed / 0 failed on main 9f586d75. The session's biggest result is a root cause: the engine integrated spectra against the CIE 1964 10° observer while the XYZ→sRGB matrix and the Jakob–Hanika upsampling table both assume CIE 1931 2°. Fixing the baked table removed a colour error that had been misread as several separate bugs — white-world tiles now land within 0.3 % of Cycles, the #795 chrome green skew drops from +8 % to +0.1 %, and the volume absorption slab moves from 0.633 to 0.678 against Cycles' 0.687. Two user-visible defects also went out: since #831 the Blender viewport had silently ignored material, light and object-move edits (hotfix #850), and the GPU had never rendered emission set on the Principled BSDF at all — in the viewport or in F12 (#835). Lamps with radius > 0 were losing energy in proportion to radius² (0.125× at Blender's default 0.1), IES now matches Cycles on both backends, GPU volumes gained blackbody emission, and volume_bounces turned out to be ignored by the engine entirely. Parked: the showcase Blender scenes were not built, and pkg265 Phase 3 is untouched.
