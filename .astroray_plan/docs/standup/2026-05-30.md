## Mid-flight (WIP, branch pushed, NOT merged)

- **pkg110 — general photon bounce** (branch `pkg110-photon-bounce`; supersedes
  the stochastic `pkg110-bsdf-photon-bounce`, now deleted).
  - A general **deterministic** refraction loop (Snell + Schlick-Fresnel, enter/exit
    from the geometric-normal sign, `iorAt(λ)` per hit, TIR) over BVH hits replaces
    the hard-coded 2-face path — any glass shape, multi-bounce, dispersion — plus a
    general (non-triangle) caster-bounds scan.
  - **Validated on a new glass-sphere scene** (`tests/test_glass_sphere_caustic.py`,
    passes): focused caustic, peak luminance 0.673, ~41× concentration. The
    deterministic refraction is proven correct (sphere focuses).
  - **Blocker (confirmed across stochastic + deterministic loops, 2-quad + solid
    prism)**: the prism `hue_spread` gate (0.70) doesn't reproduce under any general
    loop (0.0 on the 2-quad; 0.47–0.60 on a solid). Root cause: the gate relies on
    the **purity of the exactly-2-slant-face path**; a general loop on a solid also
    captures bottom/cap-scattered photons that desaturate the ROI. **Needs an OWNER
    DECISION** (re-derive the prism gate, or keep a special-case flat-caster path) —
    not a tuning fix. Options in `.astroray_plan/docs/pkg110-status-finding.md`.
  - **Rebase caveat**: this branch predates the float-param merge. When it rebases
    onto main (which now reads `caustic_boost` via `getNumber`), the glass-sphere
    scene's `set_integrator_param("caustic_boost", 14)` (int) would read as 14.0
    (10× too bright). On rebase, switch it to `set_integrator_param_float(..., 1.4)`
    and resolve the `light_tracer_caustic.cpp` constructor conflict in favour of
    `getNumber`.
