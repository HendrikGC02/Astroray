**pkg169 (PR #540, squash `b1da65f`) — two independent convictions, both
cited to PBRT-v4 §9.5 `DielectricBxDF::Sample_f` / Heitz 2018 VNDF.**

- **Conviction A (CPU, two single-scatter weight defects).** Delta glass
  dropped the Fresnel common factor: reflection returned `f=1` instead of
  `f=R`, transmission returned `f=baseColor·eta²` instead of `f=baseColor·eta²·T`
  — the pdf kept `R`/`T` so `f/pdf` never cancelled, making each interface
  lossless-in-expectation. White furnace at R=0: **1.784 → 0.990**. Separately,
  rough transmission omitted the incident cosine `|N·wi|` — `eval()` folds the
  cosine at the end via `*NdotL`, but the transmission path returns early with
  the PBRT per-steradian BTDF and nothing added the cosine back, inflating
  throughput by `1/|cosI|`. Furnace at R=0.1: **1.099 → 0.993**.
- **Conviction B (GPU, closure-graph reflection-pdf Fresnel orientation).**
  Disney glass lowers to `GMAT_CLOSURE_GRAPH`; the sampler's reflection-branch
  Fresnel used `entering = rec.normal.dot(wo) > 0` (always true) instead of
  `rec.frontFace`, computing air→glass Fresnel (small) instead of glass→air
  (~1 at total internal reflection) for internal reflections — inflating the
  pdf's denominator error up to ~20×. Fixed to `rec.frontFace` in both
  `gpu_disney_pdf` and the CPU twin `disney.cpp::pdf()`. Furnace at R=1.0:
  **2.296 → 0.930**.

Furnace after fix (256 spp, linear, band `[0.92, 1.03]`), ior 1.5:
CPU `0.990/0.990/0.993/0.980/0.926/0.902`, GPU
`0.992/0.992/0.992/0.986/0.970/0.930`; ior 1.33 both legs `0.98-0.99`.

**pkg170 (PR #542, squash `69e6d823e73a22ba11c0a61c16acba8be710acad`).** The
~2× energy gain hit **every** opaque Disney GPU material — `metallic=0` /
`transmission=0`, the default material class, so this had a far wider blast
radius than pkg169's transmission-only bug. Root cause: closure-graph `eval`
summed each lobe's **raw** weight, while `pdf` summed **normalized**
selection weights — the two didn't agree, so `f/pdf` didn't cancel per lobe.
Fix: weight each lobe by `wᵢ/W` (Veach one-sample MIS, cited), matching eval
to pdf.

**Confirmed by lobe-count arithmetic, not just band position.** A metallic
sweep isolates the mechanism directly: 2 active lobes reads **1.975**, 1
active lobe reads **0.988**, and the difference is *exactly* the extra lobe
— there's no room left for an alternative explanation.

Furnace **1.975 → 0.979–0.987**, all within `[0.92, 1.03]`. CPU control and
every neighbouring material are byte-unchanged. This PR also ships **new

that let a 2× energy-gain defect on the *default* material ship undetected.

## #541 — final resolution: PARKED (not merged)

A controlled A/B first measured **main HEAD at 0.840s vs #541 at 1.370s** —
a **1.6× wavefront perf regression**. That framed the problem as "restructure
#541 to be leaner," but the restructure attempt converged on the real root
cause: `stageAdvance`/`stageShade` are **register-saturated at 254** (device
memory `wavefront-shade-kernels-register-saturated`), and **any** correct
per-hit distinction between diffuse-colour upsample paths — not just #541's
specific implementation — spills roughly 2KB of stack. The best correct
in-kernel form measured reaches **1.222s**, still short of the 1.0s wavefront
perf ceiling.

**Correctness is solved and preserved, not lost.** A v4 of the fix is
committed **locally on the branch as `6ef2c11`, unpushed** — all correctness
gates green. The blocker is purely the register/perf ceiling, not
correctness.

**Four-way fork documented, decision deferred to the owner (TOP action
item).** The fork — (A) ship as-is and temporarily raise the perf ceiling,
(B) land a register-pressure-reduction package first and retry, (C) a
permanent re-pin of the perf gate, (D) a structural hoist of the
distinction out of the hot per-hit path — is written up in PR #541's comment
thread, with recommendation (A) noted by the implementer but the decision
explicitly left to the owner, since it trades a perf gate against a
correctness fix. **This blocks pkg172 effect (A), pkg173, and pkg168's own
closeout** — it is the single highest-priority item at hand-off.
