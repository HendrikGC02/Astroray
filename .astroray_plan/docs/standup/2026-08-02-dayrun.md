## Notes

**pkg150 (PR #536, squash `d1482a59a89076cc79a6438faf73557ac0e3a314`).**
Closed as **resolved-by-pkg149**, not implemented. pkg150's charter — VNDF
reflection was never sampleable at grazing angles, 100% of candidates
rejected — was measured on the pre-pkg149 azimuth-buggy sampler. pkg149
(#522) already fixed the underlying sampler as a side effect of its own
charter: on current main, with **zero pkg150 code**, reflection-candidate
acceptance at glass[0.3-45] is **5.1%**, and `sample()`/`pdf()` agree with
median relative error **0.0000** (N=300k). The remaining same-hemisphere
delta fallback is only **0.16%** of samples.

**The planned fix was built, measured, and deliberately not shipped.** The
spec's fix-contract option (the pbrt-v4 dead-sample technique) removes that
0.16% fallback, but it **regresses the white furnace at high roughness**
(r=1.0, CPU 0.997 -> 0.788) — the same failure mode #517 previously hit —
because the legacy delta fallback had been ad-hoc compensating for a real
gap: missing reflection-lobe multi-scatter energy, not a sampling bug. The
chi² gate's own xfail reason needed correcting too: quadrature at `ires=4`
accounts for **~90%** of the statistic, and the delta-fallback contribution
that the xfail blamed is only **2.4%** of it. The fix diff is preserved
rather than discarded, at `.astroray_plan/docs/pkg150-deadsample-fix.patch`.

**pkg166 (PR #538, squash `bfe22006554aa7007d4a8f914d5d6db6f952626b`).**
Converted 20 furnace/energy tests from `apply_gamma=True` to linear, each
with a floor+ceiling pair rather than a bare lower bound. Every band change
is either transform-justified (the linear-vs-gamma relationship for that
statistic worked out algebraically) or a strengthening (tighter, not
looser) — a Fable-5 adversarial audit specifically hunting for suspect band
moves found zero, and the merger re-ran that audit independently with the
same result.

**New guard, proven rather than assumed.** An autouse fixture now asserts
that any test named `*furnace*` or `*energy*` renders linear; a **negative**
self-test (a deliberately mis-configured furnace test) confirms the guard
actually fires rather than silently passing everything.

**Mutation check caught what gamma couldn't.** A deliberate 1.5× energy-gain
mutation injected into the metal BSDF was caught by the converted white-metal
furnace case at **1.156 > 1.02** — the same scenario under the old
gamma-clamped rendering would have read ~1.0 and passed clean. This is the
direct proof the conversion does what pkg166 was filed to do.

**Headline finding: a real, previously-invisible energy-gain defect.** The
linear conversion exposed that **Disney Principled glass transmission
creates energy**, on both backends: CPU 1.784 at the delta lobe and 1.10–1.26
across rough transmission; GPU conserving at delta but 1.10–2.30 across rough
transmission. The gamma clamp hid this for the entire life of the feature.
The three affected furnace cases are quarantined as `xfail(strict=False)` —
not silently re-pinned — pending a real fix.

**pkg156 (PR #537, squash `e2bfd8420279d80997645c91451e2ddf1a8c878a`, after a
doc-only spec-conflict resolution in merge commit `30e4cff`; code is
byte-identical to the HW-PASS-bound `155a76a4`).**

**The bounce-2 residual survived pkg120 — and pkg120 had made it worse.**
Its two-sided-MIS `w_B` leg fired **unconditionally**, including in naive
mode (`enableNEE=false`), a real regression: it was over-brightening every
naive GPU render with a visible emitter, not merely leaving pkg156's residual
alone. The fix gates that leg on `enableNEE`. The NEE path itself is
**byte-unchanged**, and pkg120's own gates were re-verified green after the
change.

**Result: depth-4 SSIM restored to 0.9955**, matching both the CPU oracle
and the pre-pkg120 wavefront.

**The gate was kept honest, not re-pinned.** The remaining ~1.4% residual is
a **different, already-known mechanism**: RGB→spectral upsampling parity,
channel-asymmetric ([1.013, 1.007, 1.014]) even under a neutral grey
background, i.e. the same shared mechanism as pkg153's R-drift. That gap is
not reachable from pkg156's scope. The gate **stays at 0.995**; restoring it
to 0.998 is now formally **BLOCKED-ON pkg168** (recorded as a marker in
pkg156's own spec by the architect) — do not re-pin without pkg168's fix.

**Independent RTX HW re-verify: PASS.** SSIM 0.99549, bit-identity exact, C2
residuals < 7.5e-08, visual inspection clean.

**Lesson recorded in pkg120's spec:** "mirror the CONDITION, not just the
term" — pkg120 correctly mirrored the MIS *term* into all 4 landing sites,
but not the *enableNEE* condition that should have gated it in the wavefront,
which is exactly the class of bug this incident was.
