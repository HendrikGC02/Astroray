### Key design decisions

- **Mechanical evidence only.** A row moves out of DROPPED-SILENT only when the scanner finds the handling in code.
  If a row is credited by hand, the lane has failed.
- **Identity is reconciled, not aliased per case.** Pick the canonical form once (Blender socket `identifier`,
  which is unique per node, as the generator should key on) and convert both sides; list every renamed key in the PR.
- **Score inputs are not re-frozen here.** The lane reports the new matrix-only ceiling with
  `coverage_report.py --score` on a scratch copy; re-freezing v4 is the lead's call after merge.
