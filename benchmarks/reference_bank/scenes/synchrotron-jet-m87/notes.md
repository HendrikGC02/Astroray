# synchrotron-jet-m87

**Vision:** An M87-like bipolar synchrotron jet — two opposing cone-shaped
emission features emerging from a central BH, viewed at 45° inclination
from the jet axis. The classic "BH with jets" iconography.

**Phase 2b status:** Same as `adaf-sgrA-faceon` — captured-not-tuned.
Owner reviews the visual at leisure; gate is loose enough to catch a
total regression (jet emission vanishes) but not aesthetics.

**Reference notes:** 256×256, 32 spp. pkg42 synchrotron plugin via
`add_black_hole(... enable_jet=True ...)`. Lorentz factor 5, opening
angle 12°, magnetic field 1e6 G, intensity_scale 5e13.
